Put your service on the referral directory

One entry is one place where people can be sent: an organisation, a palika, the services offered there and how to reach them. Fill one entry for each place you work in. A coordinator checks every entry before it appears on the public directory.

This form lists a service, not a person. No service user's name, phone number or details belong here. The contact you give in step 1 is for the coordination team to reach you and is never published; only the institutional number you give in step 4 is shown to the public.
What happens next. Your entry is saved on this phone and goes to the coordination register when there is signal. Nothing is published until a coordinator has checked it. Once listed, you will be asked to confirm the details are still correct every 30 days — the directory shows the date it was last confirmed.
