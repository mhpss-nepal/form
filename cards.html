With no signal. Open a form once while you have a connection. After that it opens and saves on the phone with no signal at all, and sends by itself the next time there is one. Nothing is lost by filling a form in a holding centre.

No names. The 5Ws form will not accept a name, a phone number, a date of birth or a national ID. If a field seems to want one, it does not — use the code.
