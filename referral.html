Where a person was sent, and whether they arrived

On 14 September the briefing recorded that around 5,000 psychosocial counselling cases had been logged, but that once a person reaches a health facility their case goes into HMIS and is no longer visible here. This form is the join. Without it, “continuum of care” is a claim rather than something anyone can show.

Field pilot · revised draft Use test data unless an approved pilot protocol says otherwise. Save keeps a device copy first. “Waiting to send” means not received; no warning is not proof of review. Confirm receipt or export before clearing.
Three kinds of case do not belong on this form. Stop and use the proper pathway.
  • Gender-based violence. GBV cases follow the specialised, survivor-centred pathway of the protection cluster, with its own consent and its own confidential system. Never record a GBV referral in a shared coordination form, not even coded.
  • An unaccompanied or separated child. These go to child-protection case management. Around 200 were reported in one area alone on 14 September; each one is a case file, not a row.
  • Immediate risk to life. Act first. Record afterwards, if at all.
Recording these here would put people at risk and would break the consent under which they spoke to you. The count of such referrals can be reported to coordination as a number, by the pathway that holds them — never as records here.

Who is being referred

No name, no phone number, no date of birth. A referral needs those to work, and they are handed over person to person or on paper between the referring worker and the receiving service. What is recorded here is only what coordination needs in order to see whether referrals are being made and completed.

Did they arrive?

This is the field that will be empty, and that is the finding. A referral is only complete when the receiving service confirms the person arrived. Nobody can fill this in from the referring side alone. Leave it as “not yet known” and come back — the proportion that stays unknown is the honest measure of whether the pathway works.
