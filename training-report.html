One record is one training or orientation event

Capacity building is not reported on the 5Ws. The people in this room are aid workers, not people reached by the response, and counting them as attendance would inflate every reach figure. They are counted here instead, as people trained.

Count the participants by the palika they work in, not by where the training was held. A training in Kathmandu for staff working in Rasuwa belongs to Rasuwa. This is what lets the coordination team see where trained workers are, and what makes the referral directory able to say a place has PFA-trained staff.
