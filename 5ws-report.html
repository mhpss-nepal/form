MHPSS activity report — the 5Ws

Who did what, where and when, and for whom. One report per session: one activity, at one place, on one day, with how many people took part. Totals are calculated in the dashboard, never entered here.

This form records services, not people. Never enter a beneficiary's name, phone number, date of birth, identity number, diagnosis, assessment score, or any detail that could identify an individual. There is no field for it, and none is to be added. If someone needs individual follow-up, refer them — do not record them here.
Works offline. Reports are saved on this phone as you submit them and sent to the coordination register on their own when you have signal. The export buttons below remain for a copy of your own.
