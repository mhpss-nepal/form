Daily / periodic MHPSS activity report

One row per activity, per site, per day. Do not add subtotal or summary rows — totals are calculated in the dashboard, never entered here.

This form records services, not people. Never enter a beneficiary's name, phone number, date of birth, identity number, diagnosis, assessment score, or any detail that could identify an individual. There is no field for it, and none is to be added. If someone needs individual follow-up, refer them — do not record them here.
Works offline. Reports are saved on this phone as you submit them and sent to the coordination register on their own when you have signal. The export buttons below remain for a copy of your own.

Seen at the session — count what you can see; already inside the total, never added to it

Five things a PFA provider can count by looking, without asking. One person can be on more than one line and is in an age group above as well; these are checked against the total but never summed with it or with each other. Leave blank what you did not see.

Reports in the current workbook carry breakdowns typed into the provider column — a “women 18+ = 12, men 18+ = 9” where an organisation name should be — because the form has nowhere else to put them. Here they have somewhere, the parts are checked against the total, and the four bands fold to the two the official 5Ws asks for.
