About this form — how it aligns with the MSP service mapping

The IASC MHPSS Minimum Service Package names a comprehensive mapping of MHPSS actors, services and activities, and joint referral pathways with a directory of services, as core coordination actions (activity 1.1, p. 27). The multi-sectoral MHPSS assessment toolkit that accompanies the MSP (field-test draft, May 2024) gives the data tool for this: Tool 3B, "Simplified MHPSS 4Ws mapping for referral pathways and service directories" (pp. 60–61), and the referral-directory fields of Tool 4 (pp. 62–63).
