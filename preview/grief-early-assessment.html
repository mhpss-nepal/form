Grief Conversation

A short conversation added to a contact you already have. It maps losses, mourning, needs and early signs of distress. It does not diagnose anyone.

1Before you start

Use this only for adults (18 and over) during a visit or session you are already doing. For a child who lost a parent or carer, follow your child protection pathway instead.

Draft wording for review
"Many families here lost someone or something important in the flood. I would like to ask a few questions about how you and your family are managing, so that we can plan support in this area. It takes about ten minutes. You can choose whether we write down your name and phone number so that we can contact you again, or keep only a code without your name. You can stop or skip any question at any time, and saying no will not change any help you receive."
Thank you. Only the palika and the date are saved, so that coordination knows how many people were approached. Nothing else is asked.

2Basic information

Name and phone number are written only if the person chooses it. They are kept by your organisation; the MHPSS coordination team can also see them to follow up. They are never published or shown on any dashboard.

5How the person has been in the past month

These are common reactions to loss. Answers are not added up and there is no score.

Ask “In the past month, how often have you had… [read each one] — not at all, on some days, or on most days?” You may use your own words.

Nepal PHQ-9 (Kohrt et al., BMC Psychiatry 2016): read each question in Nepali as written; the answers use the tool's own four-step scale. A total of 10 or more suggests follow-up by a trained provider. It is not a diagnosis.

Do not leave the person alone

Stay with the person or make sure someone they trust is with them, and talk to your supervisor today about follow-up (WHO Psychological First Aid: people who need more help).

Act today — do not leave this to the form

  • Stay with the person or make sure someone they trust is with them.
  • Call your supervisor now and arrange a same-day assessment by a trained provider.
  • If there is an immediate plan or means, follow your organisation's emergency protocol.

Follows mhGAP-HIG module SUI (self-harm/suicide). Organisation-specific numbers to be added after review.

Make sure the children have consistent care and connect the family to a child protection actor (mhGAP-HIG, grief module, p. 20).
