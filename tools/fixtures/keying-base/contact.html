One contact with one person

Use this when you have seen, spoken to or supported a specific person. For a group activity or a day's total, use the activity report instead — this form is deliberately one record per person per contact, because that is the only way a returning person can be recognised instead of counted twice.

The person's name and phone number stay on this handset. You type a few details so the phone can work out a contact code; the details are used for that calculation and then discarded. Only the code is saved. Nobody downstream — not the coordination view, not the dashboard, not this site — ever receives the name, the number, or a date of birth.
Works with no signal. Records are held on this phone as you save them and sent to the coordination register on their own when there is signal. The export remains for a copy of your own.
